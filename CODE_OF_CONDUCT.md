# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We aim to keep this project and its discussions respectful, constructive, and accessible to contributors with different experience levels.


- Be respectful and considerate.
- Focus on technical issues and helpful feedback.
- Avoid harassment, insults, discrimination, and personal attacks.
- Respect privacy: do not publish unredacted drive inventories, personal paths, or private filenames.
- Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
